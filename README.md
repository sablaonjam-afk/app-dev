# Transformers

**My favorite movie franchise**

## Overview
*Transformers* is a live-action film series based on the Hasbro toy line.  
It follows the war between the **Autobots** and the **Decepticons** on Earth.

### Main Characters
- Optimus Prime – Leader of the Autobots
- Bumblebee – The Autobot scout
- Megatron – Leader of the Decepticons
- Sam Witwicky – The human hero

## Why I Love It
1. Epic robot battles
2. Great special effects
3. Memorable soundtrack
